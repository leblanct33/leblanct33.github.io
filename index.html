1 · Requirements (AI-drafted, group-confirmed)

One question, for one decision-maker.

Should an Alberta business running vehicles between Edmonton and Calgary (a courier or trades company) do its refuelling in Edmonton?

Decision-maker

Fleet / operations manager.

Success criteria

  • Quantify the price gap
  • Test whether it is statistically real
  • Check seasonality
  • Give a dollar recommendation

Scope

Monthly regular unleaded, self-service, January 2019 to October 2026.

Deliverables

  • This published report
  • Dataset and data dictionary
  • AI review report and corrections log

9 · Analysis (MGTS-103 methods)

The gap is small, but it is real.

01

Design

Paired-sample design

Each month gives one price in each city, so we analyse the 94 monthly differences d = Calgary − Edmonton.

d̄ = 3.73¢ · s_d = 2.34 · n = 94

SE = s_d / √n = 2.34 / √94 = 0.241

02

Estimate

95% confidence interval

d̄ ± t(0.025, 93) × SE
= 3.73 ± 1.986 × 0.241
= (3.25, 4.20) ¢/L

We are 95% confident the true average gap is between 3.25 and 4.20 cents per litre.

03

Test

Paired t-test

H0: μd = 0 · H1: μd > 0

t = d̄ / SE = 3.73 / 0.241 = 15.47 · df = 93 · p < 0.0001

Reject H0: Calgary is significantly more expensive than Edmonton.

Decision
Reject H0

04

Proportion

How often Edmonton wins

p̂ = 90 / 94 = 95.7% of months

05

Relationship

Correlation and regression

Calgary = 3.96 + 0.998 × Edmonton · r = 0.995 · R² = 0.991

A slope of about 1 means the gap does not grow when prices rise (Chart E).

06

Interpretation

Small, but very consistent

The city gap is small but very consistent. Seasonality is much larger (~20¢), but a business can't easily shift when it drives.

10 · Recommendations

Fill in Edmonton when it's on the route; budget by season.

Refuel in Edmonton

Before Calgary runs, whenever practical. Saves ~3.7¢/L (CI 3.2–4.2¢).

Budget seasonally

Expect summer fuel costs ~15–20¢/L higher than December–January. Budget fuel by season, not as a flat monthly amount.

Don't detour to save

The gap is small, so fill in Edmonton only when it is already on the route.

11 · Limitations

What this data cannot tell a fleet manager.

Averages only

Monthly city averages, not station-level or daily prices.

Discounts

Fleet card discounts and individual station pricing can beat the average gap.

Policy changes

The past gap may change with taxes or market events, e.g. the 2022 provincial fuel tax relief and the 2025 removal of the consumer carbon price, which affected both cities.

Assumed fleet

The dollar estimate uses assumed fleet numbers.
